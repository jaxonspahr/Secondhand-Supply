About the concept

Secondhand should feel easier to shop.

Secondhand Supply is an online clothing concept for people who want the value of thrifting without committing an entire afternoon to the search.

Our point of view

Affordable first. Style still matters.

The idea behind Secondhand Supply is straightforward: pre-owned clothing can be practical, expressive, and affordable at the same time. The brand focuses on selecting a manageable group of pieces rather than promising a huge catalog or a luxury experience.

The website is designed to answer the questions a shopper has before deciding: What size is it? What condition is it in? Are there flaws? Is the price clear? Those details help secondhand shopping feel more predictable.
