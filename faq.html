FAQ

The details behind the pieces.

Secondhand shopping works better when shoppers know what information to expect.

Are the products real?

No. The six products currently shown are fictional placeholders created for this class project. Replace the names, photos, prices, and details with actual inventory before publishing.

How are condition and flaws described?

Each real listing should use specific condition notes and disclose visible flaws. Avoid vague claims. Include photos and measurements whenever possible.

Will every item be name-brand or vintage?

No. The concept is affordability-first. Some pieces may be recognizable, vintage-inspired, or distinctive, but name-brand and designer status should never be assumed.

What sizes will be available?

Future inventory will determine availability. The intended listing format includes the labeled size, measurements when available, and fit notes.

Do you offer shipping or local pickup?

This has not been finalized. Shipping, pickup, exchanges, and returns are placeholders and should be replaced with your actual policy before launch.

How can I ask about an item?

Use the contact page. The form is a working demo, but it does not currently send messages to a real inbox.
