The short version

Thrifting without the endless search.

Secondhand Supply is built around a simple idea: make it easier to find affordable pieces that are worth a closer look.

01

We search with a purpose.

We look for functional, wearable clothing that fits the brand’s budget-first point of view. A piece does not need to be designer or rare to be worth wearing.

02

We write down the details.

Each real listing should include size, measurements, material when known, condition, visible flaws, and fit notes. Clear information helps shoppers make better decisions online.

03

You make the final call.

Browse a smaller, clearer collection and decide what works for your wardrobe. The goal is to save time without pretending that secondhand clothing is one-size-fits-all.

What to expect

Honest details matter.

Pre-owned clothing can vary. That is part of the process, so condition notes should be specific rather than vague. A listing should tell you what is known, what is not known, and what to look at closely.

  • Measurements are provided when available.
  • Visible flaws are disclosed in the listing.
  • Sample prices and policies on this project site are placeholders.
  • Actual availability will depend on future inventory.
